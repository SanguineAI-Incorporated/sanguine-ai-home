Acoustic Inference

Perception is belief, continuously updated.
P(world state | audio history)

Acoustic Inference is not classification and not transcription. It maintains a continuously updated posterior over latent world states, where each sound acts as evidence and each moment compounds context. From this, capabilities emerge naturally: detection of distress — mechanical, environmental, and human — before explicit recognition, inference of operator intent without rigid command structure, and temporal memory that persists across time. Intelligence, in this view, is the maintenance of uncertainty. To perceive is to update belief. To act is to commit under uncertainty. This framework enables robust interaction in robotics, safety-critical systems, and autonomous environments.
